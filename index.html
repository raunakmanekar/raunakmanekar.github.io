Raunak Manekar

Assistant Professor
Department of Computer Science and Information Systems
BITS Pilani, Goa Campus, India

Raunak's research sits at the intersection of 3D vision and generative AI, with applications to perception, mapping, and teleoperation for embodied agents — from autonomous vehicles to humanoid robots. Before joining BITS Goa, he was a Senior AI Scientist at GE Healthcare in Bengaluru, working on MRI technologies. He earned his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Minnesota Twin Cities under Prof. Jaideep Srivastava, then worked as a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. John Miao's Coherent Imaging Group at UCLA.

3D Vision & Persistent Mapping for Embodied Agents
Developing algorithms for long-horizon, persistent scene representations that enable autonomous vehicles and humanoid robots to build and maintain reliable 3D maps of dynamic environments.
Perception for Autonomous Driving & Robotic Teleoperation
Predictive display and perception systems that make autonomous vehicles and remotely operated robots safer and more reliable, supported by ANRF PM-ECRG funding.
Generative AI & Computational Imaging
Learning-based approaches to image reconstruction and computational imaging — phase retrieval, low-dose sensing, and related inverse problems — using generative models and deep priors.
AI for Reasoning & Foundation Models
Exploring how large foundation models reason about structure, space, and causality, and how these capabilities can be grounded in embodied, real-world settings.
